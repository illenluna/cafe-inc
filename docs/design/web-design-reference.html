The visual system for cafe-inc's web presence, distilled from the studio mood board at docs/design/references/1.png: chamfered geometry, oxblood and olive held against warm paper, and one clear ritual per page.

01 · Mood & principles

What the board is actually saying

Underneath the espresso shots and cracked-glaze macros, the same four decisions repeat. They're the rules everything below is built from.

P.01

Cut, not curved

Every corner is chamfered, never rounded. Softness comes from paper color and photography — not border-radius.

P.02

One dark, one bright

Each screen pairs a single deep surface — ink or oxblood — with cream paper. Olive and rose are moves, never backgrounds.

P.03

Type stands at attention

Display type is tight, uppercase, heavy. Body copy is quieter and looser, so the two never compete for the same job.

P.04

Shoot the ritual

Photography favors macro texture and mid-pour motion over static product shots — the moment, not the merchandise.

02 · Color

Five surfaces, used on purpose

Cream carries most of the site. Ink and oxblood are the two dark surfaces — pick one per section, don't mix them in the same view. Olive and rose are accents only: tags, focus states, small fills.

Cream
#F1E7D2
Primary paper. Default background for reading-heavy sections.
Ink
#20140D
Darkest surface and default text color on cream. Use for nav, footer, hero.
Oxblood
#3B1015
Second dark surface — moodier, warmer than ink. Feature sections, plaques.
Olive
#57612E
Accent fill for tags and secondary interactive states. Never a full-page background.
Rose
#E2A7AC
Warmest accent — focus rings, highlighted links, seasonal tags. Use sparingly.
Sand
#C7A97C
Hairline rule and border color on dark surfaces; muted label text.

03 · Typography

Three jobs, three faces

DisplayUnbounded / PP Neue Machina, 800–900
Order ahead

Uppercase, tight tracking (‑2%), used for headlines and short labels only — never for paragraphs. This document renders it with a system bold sans fallback; specify the weight above in production.

BodyFraunces / Canela, regular & italic

A warm, slightly wonky serif for anything meant to be read, not scanned — the story behind a roast, a location note, an about page. It sits quietly next to the loud display face on purpose.

04 · Mark

Wordmark & glyph

CAFÉINC.

The wordmark is two solid blocks in cream on oxblood, always chamfered with the standard cut-md value. Clearspace equals the height of one block on every side. Minimum width: 96px — below that, use the glyph alone.

  • Don't round the plaque's corners
  • Don't recolor the mark outside cream / oxblood / ink
  • Don't add drop shadows or gradients to the plaque
  • Don't stretch the blocks to fill unrelated widths

Cup glyph — favicon & app icon use

07 · Imagery

Shoot direction

Three placeholder textures stand in for real photography. Favor natural light, shallow depth of field, and motion over posed product shots.

Macro texture — crumb, glaze, steam

08 · Motion

Quiet by default

Hover

Cut-corner cards and buttons lift 2px with no shadow blur increase — a nudge, not a bounce.

Links

Underline draws in from the left on hover/focus, 150ms ease. No color-only hover states.

Page load

Hero plaque and headline fade up in one short staggered pass (under 500ms total). Nothing else animates on load.

Reduced motion

All transitions and entrance animation are removed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — content appears instantly, never hidden waiting on JS.

09 · Voice

Say what happens, plainly

Active voice, no filler, tone warm but terse. A button's label carries through to its confirmation.

Avoid

Use

Avoid

10 · Site patterns

Added during the real build

The v1 system above didn't anticipate every real page. These patterns were added while building the actual cafe-inc site — they follow the same rules (cut corners, mono labels, one dark surface per section) and should be treated as canonical, not as drift.

Field — select / textarea / date / time

Section 06 only showed a text input. Forms with real choices (party size, reservation date/time, admin fields) extend .field to selects, textareas, and native date/time inputs with the same border, background, and focus ring — plus a custom chevron on select so its arrow doesn't clash with the chamfered aesthetic.

Dark-band button contrast

.btn--secondary and .btn--ghost use ink-colored text, which nearly disappears on .band--ink/.band--oxblood. On those bands they automatically swap to cream/rose — no new component, just a band-aware override.

Item card — photo menu card

Used for the Home "most-ordered" grid and the full /cardapio page: a photo (or a fallback imagery tile when the real photo isn't sourced yet), name, price, description, and selo tags.
